## Depth Anything V2 Small (Core ML)

Portrait Maker includes Apple's Core ML conversion of Depth Anything V2 Small,
downloaded at build time from https://huggingface.co/apple/coreml-depth-anything-v2-small
(commit cfef6f6f2a70783dedc0bfae40cecbc2052285d3).

- Model: Depth Anything V2, by Lihe Yang et al. (https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09414)
- License: Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)

The model is used without changes.

## Sample portrait

`Resources/SamplePortrait.jpg` is a photo of a plaster bust of Robert Burns (1843) by
John Crookshanks King, in the Smithsonian American Art Museum. The photo is from
Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Robert_Burns_Bust.jpg),
under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). It is scaled to 960 pixels wide.
